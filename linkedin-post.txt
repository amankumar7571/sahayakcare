💡 *My Project:*
SahayakCare

🔍 *Problem Statement:*
Elderly Care & Medication Management Platform

🛠️ *What I Built:*
SahayakCare is a passcode-protected shared care platform that helps an elderly person, their family and caretakers manage medications, appointments and daily health routines in one place. It tracks medication schedules with 7-day adherence scoring and streaks, manages doctor appointments, lets new care-circle members join only with the elderly person's approval, logs vitals, and drafts a caretaker email in one tap when a dose is missed or an appointment is near — going beyond a basic reminder app to keep the whole family informed.

Medication schedules — 4 daily slots, stock tracking and low-stock alerts.
Adherence tracking — today's doses, 7-day ring, streaks and most-missed slot insight.
Appointments — upcoming visits with reminders.
Care circle — family and doctors join only after the owner's approval.
Vitals log — blood pressure, sugar, weight, pulse.
Smart reminders — overdue and due-soon alerts, one-tap email to caretakers.
Secure and synced — passcode-protected, works offline, cloud sync across devices.
Made for elders — Hindi/English toggle and large-text mode.

💻 *Tech Stack:*
Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, HTML, localStorage frontend + FastAPI, SQLite and JWT backend on Render, with live cloud sync.
